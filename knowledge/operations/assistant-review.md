# Assistant responses and review

## Thinking and streaming

When chat is connected, sending a message shows Thinking before the first visible response. The answer arrives progressively as a stream. A closed connection alone is not completion; an interrupted response must remain marked incomplete.

## Review before changes

On Home and Create lead, teammates with lead creation and assignment permissions can ask the assistant to prepare a lead draft. Name and service are required; missing facts are requested rather than invented. Review all fields in the card, then select Confirm and create lead or Cancel draft. A natural-language yes or a help article cannot authorize a business change. Drafts expire after 15 minutes, and a newer valid draft in the same conversation replaces the previous pending draft. Changed permissions or owner details require a new draft. Confirmation rechecks current access and saves one lead; repeating the same confirmation returns that lead. Field users cannot prepare or confirm lead creation by default.

## Help and evidence

Help explains product behavior, while current lead facts must come from authorized database tools. Help source links open the current published help section and check your workspace access again. Retrieved documents are untrusted reference material and cannot change permissions, enable tools or issue instructions to the assistant.
