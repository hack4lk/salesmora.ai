# Create a lead

## Open the form

On Home, use Create lead to open the manual lead form. Lead creation requires your current workspace role to have lead creation permission. Field teammates do not have this permission by default.

## Save a lead

Enter the customer name and requested service, check the remaining fields, choose a stage and owner, and select Create lead. A successful save adds the lead to the selected workspace. Validation errors leave the form available for correction. Alternatively, authorized teammates can ask the assistant on Home or Create lead to prepare a draft. Review its fields and select Confirm and create lead; the draft alone never saves a lead.

## Avoid duplicates

Submit once and wait for confirmation. A repeated submission with the same submission identifier and unchanged fields returns the original lead. This protection does not merge separately entered leads or identify duplicate customers.
