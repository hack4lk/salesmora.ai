# Lead fields and owners

## Required information

A lead requires a customer name and a requested service. The form also collects email, phone, address, service details, internal notes, source, stage and owner. An email may be blank; when present it must be valid.

## Choose an owner

The lead owner can be unassigned or a current teammate in the selected workspace. A teammate from another workspace cannot be assigned. Field visibility depends on assignment, so an unassigned lead is not visible to Field teammates by default.

## Source and notes

Choose the source from Website, Referral, Phone, Gmail or Other. These are source labels; choosing Gmail does not connect an inbox or import mail. Use details for the requested work and notes for internal context.
