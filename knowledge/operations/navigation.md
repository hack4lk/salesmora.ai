# Navigate your workspace

## Home and workspace

Home and the assistant launcher are available to all four roles by default. Individual widgets still require their own permissions. Always confirm the selected workspace before working with records; workspaces have separate memberships and policies.

## Available menu items

The left menu follows your current role permissions. Some permitted future features are labelled Coming soon and cannot be opened yet. Settings opens the default settings page; Permissions appears beneath it when allowed. A missing menu item cannot be unlocked by changing a URL.

## Open the assistant

Use the floating assistant callout at the bottom right to open the dark-blue chat modal. Closing the modal hides it; Stop is the separate control for ending a running response. When live chat is enabled, the assistant explains current product help and reads your permitted Home pipeline counts. Chat sending can be disabled by server configuration. On Home and Create lead, authorized teammates can also prepare a lead draft and create it using the separate Confirm and create lead button. Other record changes remain unavailable.
