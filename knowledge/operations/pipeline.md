# Understand the lead pipeline

## Pipeline stages

The current lead stages are New, Contacted, Estimate sent and In progress. New is the initial enquiry; Contacted indicates contact was made; Estimate sent records that an estimate was sent; In progress indicates active work. Choosing a stage does not itself send an estimate or start a job.

## Counts and visibility

The Home pipeline widget counts leads in each stage within the selected workspace. Owner, Admin and Office see workspace totals by default. Field teammates see only leads assigned to them. A zero count may reflect assignment restrictions rather than an empty workspace.

## Current records

Pipeline counts are current CRM facts read from the workspace database with your permissions. Help articles explain the stages and do not contain customer records or live totals. The leads list and stage editing are planned surfaces; use the currently available Home widget and creation form.
