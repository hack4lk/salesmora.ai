Questions

Getting to know SalesMora.

Who is SalesMora for?

SalesMora is designed for small and midsize service businesses, including home-service teams such as contractors, plumbers, HVAC technicians, and electricians.

How does the AI sidekick help?

The assistant can help you find information and draft supported workflows, such as a reminder for an approved estimate that still needs a visit. You review a workflow before publishing it. Availability and plan details will be confirmed for launch.

How do I get started?

Create an account and follow the steps to set up your business workspace. This prototype simulates registration and does not create an account.

Which plan should I choose?

Free, Standard, and Premium are intended for different stages of business growth. The prices shown are draft and will be confirmed for launch.
